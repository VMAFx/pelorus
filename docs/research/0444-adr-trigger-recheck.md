# Re-check of ADR revisit triggers

**Date:** 2026-10-28

**Issue:** [#444](https://github.com/VMAFx/pelorus/issues/444).

**Scope:** Revisit triggers in [ADR-0110](../adr/0110-avoption-control-plane-contract.md), [ADR-0123](../adr/0123-anime-dehalo.md), [ADR-0124](../adr/0124-anime-aa.md), [ADR-0127](../adr/0127-deblock.md), [ADR-0131](../adr/0131-mc-denoise-warp.md), [ADR-0182](../adr/0182-vulkan-qpmap-fill-paths.md), and [ADR-0184](../adr/0184-vulkan-drm-modifier-output-pools.md) on master `6a9bd56`.

## Verdict

Several early Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) recorded explicit revisit triggers—conditions under which alternative designs, deferred features, or default choices should be reconsidered. Across the seven ADRs evaluated on 2026-10-28 on master `6a9bd56`, only two triggers have fired or partially fired:

1. **ADR-0110 (fired)**: The knob and filter inventory expanded from 1 filter (10 frozen deband knobs) to 10 filters plus `pelorus_fgs` BSF. The trigger was re-decided on 2026-10-15 by [ADR-0196](../adr/0196-filter-knob-contracts.md) via per-filter machine-readable JSON contracts (`docs/api/knobs/<filter>.json`) with drift gating in `make docs-check` (`scripts/check-filter-knob-contracts.py`). The deband contract in [docs/api/control-plane.md](../api/control-plane.md) remains in force.
2. **ADR-0184 (fired in part)**: Stock FFmpeg defects 1, 2, and partial defect 3 (`02313`) are resolved in the shared series `v0.5.0` (patches 0004, 0005, 0006, 0035). However, five export-path validation errors (`03909`, `03874`, `03875`, `00049`, `03254`) remain unfixed without patches, and the primary architectural rationale (preserving input-pool reuse across Vulkan decode-to-encode pipelines) holds regardless. DRM tiling remains opt-in; the revisit trigger is narrowed to the remaining VUIDs.
3. **ADRs 0123, 0124, 0127, 0131, 0182 (not fired)**: Classical algorithm tuning continues for dehalo (PR [#409](https://github.com/VMAFx/pelorus/pull/409) / [ADR-0226](../adr/0226-dehalo-feathered-mask.md)) with on-content proof pending the pinned corpus ([#135](https://github.com/VMAFx/pelorus/issues/135)); aa, deblock, and mc warp quality evaluations (QE-05, QE-06, QE-09) remain open in [#202](https://github.com/VMAFx/pelorus/issues/202) without evidence showing the classical approaches insufficient or exhibiting block-edge artefacts; and tested GPU drivers neither lack `LINEAR` QP map support nor provide `COMPUTE` capabilities on video encode queue families.

## Method

Each target ADR was inspected for explicit trigger sentences matching the pattern `revisit`, `re-evaluate`, or `reconsider` qualified by `when`, `once`, `if`, or `after`. Each trigger was then checked against the verified repository state, commit history, build configuration, and host capabilities on 2026-10-28 on master `6a9bd56`.
