# Research 0607: human-vision leads for the pre-encode filters

**Date:** 2026-11-12

**Scope:** a literature survey of human visual system (HVS) mechanisms that a pre-encode filter can use, mapped onto the Pelorus filters as they stand at `5f2adde` (FFmpeg `n9.0.2`). It covers six topics: luminance adaptation and dark-scene banding, noise and grain perception, masking-driven bit allocation, foveation and saliency, temporal flicker sensitivity, and colour opponency. Nothing here is measured. Every lead is a hypothesis with a pre-registered claim and a kill criterion, and each needs its own bench run before it can change a default.

**Marks.** `[V]`: the DOI or arXiv id was resolved on 2026-11-12 and the title, first author, year and venue below come from that record. `(U)`: not resolved. Every source in the lead tables is `[V]`. Sources the drafting step could not resolve were dropped and are listed under [Method](#method).

Related: [competitive audit](0172-competitive-audit.md), [roadmap inventory](0172-roadmap-inventory.md), [smart deband](0101-smart-deband.md), [step-gated dither](0283-deband-step-gated-dither.md), [analyze per-cell maps](0177-analyze-per-cell-maps.md), [grain estimate accuracy](0203-grain-estimate-accuracy.md).

## Verdict

The survey drafted 36 leads. F5 merged into B3, which leaves 35 rows in the tables. One of them (F1) is already answered, so 34 leads are open. Checking each against the code sorted them as follows:

- **Answered already (1).** F1, per-frame against static dither seeding, was measured in [research 0283](0283-deband-step-gated-dither.md) and decided in [ADR-0225](../adr/0225-deband-step-gated-dither.md). NVENC quantises per-frame hashed dither away, so static ordered dither is pinned.
- **Partly present (3).** M6 (motion confidence in temporal denoise), C1 (separate chroma strength) and M4 (texture-damped denoise) extend options that exist today.
- **Need a decision first (4).** A6, C3, M5 and the PQ half of A4 need an HDR-out recipe. [ADR-0237](../adr/0237-tonemap-stage-placement.md) tone-maps HDR to SDR before the Pelorus filters, and it rejected PQ-input filtering for that path.
- **Behind the ADR-0135 bar (6).** M1, M2, M3, S1, S2 and S5 steer bits by a spatial perceptual map. [ADR-0135](../adr/0135-analyze-perceptual-aq-map.md) closed that family against encoder variance-AQ. The only revival path it names is a structure-tensor or oriented-edge discriminator plus a CSF term (ADR-0135, lines 78 to 98).

The [shortlist](#ranked-shortlist) puts the cheapest testable leads first:

1. C1, a sweep of the existing chroma denoise options.
2. F6, temporal smoothing of grain parameters.
3. B3, a closed denoise and grain-estimate loop.
4. A1 with A2, a banding-visibility map that deband consumes per cell.
5. A4, an SDR dark-scene deband threshold.

## Method

1. Three parallel drafting sessions each took two topics from one brief and returned leads in one fixed shape: mechanism, source, filter, effort, claim with kill criterion, and risk. The author judged every draft. Each DOI was re-resolved through OpenAlex (`batch_resolve_references`) and each arXiv id through the arXiv abstract service, and every title, first author, year and venue was compared with the draft. All 39 distinct source ids in the tables matched.
2. Dropped because no record resolved for them: the Barten 1999 book, Watson's 1986 handbook chapter, Miller 2013 on PQ, Norkin's AV1 film-grain paper and the original S-CIELAB paper by Zhang and Wandell. One drafting session's first DOI for Mullen 1985 pointed to an unrelated paper; the session caught it, and the record in the table is the corrected one. That is why the survey cites only resolved records.
3. The repository check read the AVOption tables in `ffmpeg-patches/files/vf_pelorus_*_vulkan.c` and the matching shaders in `ffmpeg-patches/files/vulkan/`. The [existing behaviour](#existing-behaviour-that-shapes-the-leads) section lists what it found. Option names in the tables come from those files, and "new option" means no option exists.
4. Effort: **S** = host-side or option-only change; **M** = shader change with no interop ABI change; **L** = new shader stage or new interop section (append-only, `PELORUS_ABI_MINOR` bump).
5. Claims name a metric from the vmafx oracle where one fits (CAMBI, SSIMULACRA2, VMAF, XPSNR, BD-rate), a content class, a direction, a threshold and a kill criterion. CAMBI deltas smaller than the encoder noise floor of research 0283 (hevc 2.90 to 3.34, av1 2.43 to 2.49 across seeds) are not results.

### 3. Masking-driven bit allocation

The bar from ADR-0135 applies to every lead that steers QP. Variance-AQ is a scalar log-variance law. It cannot tell ordered high-variance structure (edges, periodic texture, poor maskers) from disordered stochastic texture (good maskers) at the same variance. A structure-aware map can only win on content where both classes exist at equal variance. Elsewhere it should be expected to lose, as ADR-0135 measured. Each of M1 to M3 therefore starts with a discriminator pre-step: the M2 tile classification accuracy.
