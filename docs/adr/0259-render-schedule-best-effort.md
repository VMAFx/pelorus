# ADR-0259: Keep the hourly changelog render schedule under best-effort platform delivery

- **Status**: Accepted
- **Date**: 2026-11-16
- **Deciders**: Lusoris
- **Tags**: `release`, `ci`
- **Related**: [ADR-0252](0252-scheduled-changelog-render-pull-request.md) (amended), [ADR-0251](0251-changelog-render-owed-on-pull-requests.md), [ADR-0245](0245-changelog-render-at-landing.md)

## Context

`.github/workflows/changelog-render-pr.yml` ([ADR-0252](0252-scheduled-changelog-render-pull-request.md)) landed on `master` at `2026-11-10T08:15:12Z` (commit `cee7cb7`, pull request #592) with cron `41 * * * *` (UTC) and `--open-after-hours 6`. Issue #631 observed that scheduled runs started roughly every 6 h rather than hourly. Over `2026-11-10T08:15:00Z`..`2026-11-16T09:00:00Z`, the workflow remained active, its cron syntax was valid, and `0` scheduled runs concluded `cancelled` across all three scheduled workflows in `VMAFx/pelorus`, ruling out concurrency-group cancellation (`changelog-render-pr` sets `cancel-in-progress: false`). The workflow started `22` of `145` hourly slots (`15.2%`), with gaps between started runs of `4.10` h minimum, `6.94` h median and `8.92` h maximum. The sibling scheduled workflows were delayed and dropped the same way: `changelog-staleness.yml` (`17 */6 * * *`) started `13` of `24` slots, and every daily `tester-publish.yml` slot (`17 2 * * *`) started once but `6.8` to `7.7` h late. Per-run timestamps, lateness and the slot counting rule are in the [measurement digest](../research/0631-render-schedule-delivery.md) Tables A and C.

Replaying `decide_render_pr` in `scripts/release/check-changelog-staleness.py` at `now = created_at` across all `24` render workflow runs (`22` `schedule`, `2` `workflow_dispatch`) matched the workflow's `mint` step execution on `23` of `24` runs; the sole mismatch was the first scheduled run `42856098380` (`2026-11-10T16:35:46Z`, head `1f434b47c4d6`), where `decide` emitted `open=true` (`11.9` h owed since `38861a659e50`) and `scripts/release/open-render-pr.py --check-settings` failed as designed before `RELEASE_BOT_CLIENT_ID` and `RELEASE_BOT_PRIVATE_KEY` were configured. GitHub's `events-that-trigger-workflows#schedule` documentation (read 2026-11-16) states in substance that scheduled workflows can be delayed under high GitHub Actions load, that the start of every hour experiences peak load, that severe load can drop queued scheduled jobs altogether, that picking another minute only lowers the likelihood of delay, and that public repositories disable scheduled workflows after 60 days without repository activity. Sparse starts on `changelog-render-pr.yml` therefore reflect platform best-effort schedule delivery rather than a workflow or checker defect.

## Decision

We will keep `.github/workflows/changelog-render-pr.yml` unchanged at cron `41 * * * *` and `scripts/release/check-changelog-staleness.py --open-after-hours 6`, and amend [ADR-0252](0252-scheduled-changelog-render-pull-request.md) to record best-effort schedule delivery. Because the `decide` step computes owed render age from git history (`now - first_fragment_committer_time`) rather than counting elapsed cron windows, a dropped cron slot never loses a due render pull request; it delays pull request creation until the next started run that passes the settings check and opens the pull request. A started run that fails first, as run `42856098380` did before the release-bot settings existed, extends the delay to the next successful run or a manual `workflow_dispatch`. Scheduled open time is therefore `6 h + gap` to the next successful run. In the measured sample the maximum gap between started runs was `8.92` h (between runs `43185965423` and `43272459556`), giving a worst-case scheduled open time of `14.92` h: `5.08` h of margin before the `20` h pull request gate ([ADR-0251](0251-changelog-render-owed-on-pull-requests.md)) and `9.08` h before the `24` h default-branch push gate ([ADR-0245](0245-changelog-render-at-landing.md)). These margins exclude runner queue wait; with the measured maximum of `21.97` min (run `43785808315`), the sample's worst case is `15.29` h and the margins are `4.71` h and `8.71` h. These figures describe the measured sample, not a limit: GitHub sets no upper bound on schedule delay or drops, so a longer gap can exceed them. If a schedule gap plus runner queue wait ever exceeds `14` h after a fragment reaches `6` h owed, ADR-0251 fails non-render pull requests in `ci.yml` (`docs` job) with remedy `land the render PR first`, and ADR-0245 fails pushes past `24` h; both gates remain the backstop, and maintainers can trigger `workflow_dispatch` or render by hand (`bash scripts/release/concat-changelog-fragments.sh --write`) as documented in `changelog.d/README.md`.

## Alternatives considered

| Option | Pros | Cons | Why not chosen |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Accept best-effort platform delivery; keep `41 * * * *` and `--open-after-hours 6` (chosen) | Zero workflow, script, or credential changes; state-based `decide` recovers on the next started run; `14.92` h measured worst-case open keeps `5.08` h margin below `20` h | Render pull requests open at `6` h plus the platform schedule gap rather than strictly at `6` h | — |
| Lower `--open-after-hours` (for example to `3` h; runner-up) | Adds `3` h of schedule-gap margin (`17` h instead of `14` h before ADR-0251's `20` h limit) | Cuts the fragment batching window in half during active landing waves, opening more bot pull requests and running the 16 required checks and strict up-to-date re-renders more often | Measured worst-case open (`14.92` h) already clears `20` h by `5.08` h, and the episodes that exceeded `20` h were held in merge wait after opening, not by the `6` h threshold |
| Multiple cron entries per hour in `changelog-render-pr.yml` | More nominal trigger opportunities per hour without external infrastructure | Runs on the same GitHub Actions scheduler whose load delayed or dropped `41 * * * *`, `17 */6 * * *`, and `17 2 * * *`; fires redundant runs when load is low; requires widening `scripts/check-build-config.py` | Does not bound platform scheduler drops during load spikes and wastes runner minutes during normal periods |
| External timer service invoking `workflow_dispatch` | Bypasses GitHub's `schedule` trigger queue | Requires an off-forge token with `actions: write` (an operator or App credential outside GitHub) plus a separate hosted timer to run and monitor | Adds an external credential and service for latency variance already bounded by ADR-0251 and ADR-0245 |
| Push-triggered `decide` step when fragments land on `master` | Starts immediately on `master` pushes without waiting for cron | A newly landed first fragment is `0.0` h owed at push time (`< 6` h), so `decide_render_pr` emits `open=false`; quiet periods after a batch still rely on `schedule` | Fails to open a render pull request after a quiet 6 h batch window, which is the case the schedule exists to handle |

## Consequences

- **Positive**: `.github/workflows/changelog-render-pr.yml`, `scripts/release/check-changelog-staleness.py`, `scripts/release/open-render-pr.py`, and `scripts/check-build-config.py` remain unchanged with zero additional credentials or runner load. State-based age checks tolerate arbitrary dropped cron slots without losing owed renders, and when squash auto-merge remained armed (pull requests #625, #627, #629), render debt cleared end-to-end at `6.9`–`9.0` h owed (`0.41`–`0.57` h after pull request creation).
- **Negative**: ADR-0252's consequence statement ("appears 6 h after the first unrendered fragment") holds only under uninterrupted hourly delivery; across `2026-11-10`..`2026-11-16`, scheduled render pull requests opened at up to `14.0` h owed (episode `832b020a4a35`, pull request #605, `7.04` h of schedule delay) with a worst case of `14.92` h in the measured sample (`6 h + 8.92 h` max schedule gap), or `15.29` h with the `21.97` min maximum runner queue wait on run `43785808315`. A schedule gap plus runner queue wait above `14` h after a fragment reaches `6` h owed lets ADR-0251 fail other pull requests at `20` h owed (and above `18` h lets ADR-0245 fail pushes at `24` h owed).
- **Neutral / follow-ups**: across the six owed episodes in `2026-11-10`..`2026-11-16` ([digest Table B](../research/0631-render-schedule-delivery.md)), each episode lost `0.17` to `7.04` h to schedule delay (dropped or late slots, which the run data cannot tell apart), and the breaches of `20` h and `24` h came from merge wait rather than schedule delivery:
  - PR #595 opened at `13.6` h owed, after the first scheduled run `42856098380` failed its settings check before the release-bot settings existed, and merged at `14.1` h owed after `plurano-merge-train` disabled auto-merge once (`0.55` h merge wait).
  - PR #605 opened at `14.0` h owed and merged at `27.0` h owed (`12.96` h merge wait): `plurano-merge-train` disabled auto-merge (`manually_disabled`) after each of the three `vmafx-release-bot` arms, and during that hold `ci.yml` push run `43371294722` failed step `Changelog render owed at most 24 h` at `25.6` h owed.
  - PR #613 opened at `7.7` h owed and merged at `25.0` h owed (`17.32` h merge wait) after the same auto-merge disables on all three arms.
  - PRs #625, #627 and #629 opened at `8.6`, `6.4` and `8.4` h owed with auto-merge never disabled, and `vmafx-release-bot` merged them `0.41` to `0.57` h later (`6.9` to `9.0` h owed).
