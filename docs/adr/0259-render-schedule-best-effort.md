# ADR-0259: Keep the hourly changelog render schedule under best-effort platform delivery

- **Status**: Accepted
- **Date**: 2026-11-16
- **Deciders**: Lusoris
- **Tags**: `release`, `ci`
- **Related**: [ADR-0252](0252-scheduled-changelog-render-pull-request.md) (amended), [ADR-0251](0251-changelog-render-owed-on-pull-requests.md), [ADR-0245](0245-changelog-render-at-landing.md)

## Context

`.github/workflows/changelog-render-pr.yml` ([ADR-0252](0252-scheduled-changelog-render-pull-request.md)) landed on `master` at `2026-11-10T08:15:12Z` (commit `cee7cb7`, pull request #592) with cron `41 * * * *` (UTC) and `--open-after-hours 6`. Issue #631 observed that scheduled runs started roughly every 6 h rather than hourly. Over `2026-11-10T08:15:00Z`..`2026-11-16T09:00:00Z` ([measurement digest](../research/0631-render-schedule-delivery.md)), the workflow remained active, its cron syntax was valid, and `0` scheduled runs concluded `cancelled` across all three scheduled workflows in `VMAFx/pelorus`, ruling out concurrency-group cancellation (`changelog-render-pr` sets `cancel-in-progress: false`). Over that window, `.github/workflows/changelog-render-pr.yml` (`41 * * * *`) started `22` of `145` hourly slots (`15.2%`), with inter-run schedule gaps of `4.10` h min / `6.94` h median / `8.92` h max (`8.34` h initial wait from `2026-11-10T08:15:12Z` to first scheduled run `42856098380`) and lower-bound lateness of `2.6` / `27.6` / `59.6` min after the preceding `:41` slot. The sibling scheduled workflows experienced comparable platform drops and delays over the same window: `.github/workflows/changelog-staleness.yml` (`17 */6 * * *`) started `13` of `24` slots (`54.2%`, max gap `13.18` h, lateness lower bound `10.2` / `57.5` / `349.0` min), and `.github/workflows/tester-publish.yml` (`17 2 * * *`) started `6` scheduled runs against `6` daily slots (`100.0%`, including `42795224086` from the `2026-11-10T02:17:00Z` slot while the `2026-11-16T02:17:00Z` slot started after `09:00:00Z` as `43843803978`) with lower-bound lateness of `407.4` / `441.4` / `461.9` min and max gap `24.51` h.

Replaying `decide_render_pr` in `scripts/release/check-changelog-staleness.py` at `now = created_at` across all `24` render workflow runs (`22` `schedule`, `2` `workflow_dispatch`) matched the workflow's `mint` step execution on `23` of `24` runs; the sole mismatch was the first scheduled run `42856098380` (`2026-11-10T16:35:46Z`, head `1f434b47c4d6`), where `decide` emitted `open=true` (`11.9` h owed since `38861a659e50`) and `scripts/release/open-render-pr.py --check-settings` failed as designed before `RELEASE_BOT_CLIENT_ID` and `RELEASE_BOT_PRIVATE_KEY` were configured. GitHub's `events-that-trigger-workflows#schedule` documentation (read 2026-11-16) states in substance that scheduled workflows can be delayed under high GitHub Actions load, that the start of every hour experiences peak load, that severe load can drop queued scheduled jobs altogether, that picking another minute only lowers the likelihood of delay, and that public repositories disable scheduled workflows after 60 days without repository activity. Sparse starts on `changelog-render-pr.yml` therefore reflect platform best-effort schedule delivery rather than a workflow or checker defect.

## Decision

We will keep `.github/workflows/changelog-render-pr.yml` unchanged at cron `41 * * * *` and `scripts/release/check-changelog-staleness.py --open-after-hours 6`, and amend [ADR-0252](0252-scheduled-changelog-render-pull-request.md) to record best-effort schedule delivery. Because the `decide` step computes owed render age from git history (`now - first_fragment_committer_time`) rather than counting elapsed cron windows, a dropped cron slot never loses a due render pull request; it delays pull request creation only until the next started run. Worst-case scheduled open time is `6 h + gap` between started runs: at the measured maximum inter-run gap of `8.92` h (between runs `43185965423` and `43272459556`), worst-case scheduled open time is `14.92` h (plus runner queue wait, measured up to `21.97` min on run `43785808315`), leaving `5.08` h of margin before the `20` h pull request gate ([ADR-0251](0251-changelog-render-owed-on-pull-requests.md)) and `9.08` h before the `24` h default-branch push gate ([ADR-0245](0245-changelog-render-at-landing.md)). If a schedule gap ever exceeds `14` h after a fragment reaches `6` h owed, ADR-0251 fails non-render pull requests in `ci.yml` (`docs` job) with remedy `land the render PR first`, and ADR-0245 fails pushes past `24` h; both gates remain the backstop, and maintainers can trigger `workflow_dispatch` or render by hand (`bash scripts/release/concat-changelog-fragments.sh --write`) as documented in `changelog.d/README.md`.

## Alternatives considered

| Option | Pros | Cons | Why not chosen |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Accept best-effort platform delivery; keep `41 * * * *` and `--open-after-hours 6` (chosen) | Zero workflow, script, or credential changes; state-based `decide` recovers on the next started run; `14.92` h worst-case open keeps `5.08` h margin below `20` h | Render pull requests open at `6` h plus the platform schedule gap rather than strictly at `6` h | — |
| Lower `--open-after-hours` (for example to `3` h; runner-up) | Adds `3` h of schedule-gap margin (`17` h instead of `14` h before ADR-0251's `20` h limit) | Cuts the fragment batching window in half during active landing waves, opening more bot pull requests and running the 16 required checks and strict up-to-date re-renders more often | Measured worst-case open (`14.92` h) already clears `20` h by `5.08` h, and the episodes that exceeded `20` h were held in merge wait after opening, not by the `6` h threshold |
| Multiple cron entries per hour in `changelog-render-pr.yml` | More nominal trigger opportunities per hour without external infrastructure | Runs on the same GitHub Actions scheduler whose load delayed or dropped `41 * * * *`, `17 */6 * * *`, and `17 2 * * *`; fires redundant runs when load is low; requires widening `scripts/check-build-config.py` | Does not bound platform scheduler drops during load spikes and wastes runner minutes during normal periods |
| External timer service invoking `workflow_dispatch` | Bypasses GitHub's `schedule` trigger queue | Requires an off-forge token with `actions: write` (an operator or App credential outside GitHub) plus a separate hosted timer to run and monitor | Adds an external credential and service for latency variance already bounded by ADR-0251 and ADR-0245 |
| Push-triggered `decide` step when fragments land on `master` | Starts immediately on `master` pushes without waiting for cron | A newly landed first fragment is `0.0` h owed at push time (`< 6` h), so `decide_render_pr` emits `open=false`; quiet periods after a batch still rely on `schedule` | Fails to open a render pull request after a quiet 6 h batch window, which is the case the schedule exists to handle |

## Consequences

- **Positive**: `.github/workflows/changelog-render-pr.yml`, `scripts/release/check-changelog-staleness.py`, `scripts/release/open-render-pr.py`, and `scripts/check-build-config.py` remain unchanged with zero additional credentials or runner load. State-based age checks tolerate arbitrary dropped cron slots without losing owed renders, and when squash auto-merge remained armed (pull requests #625, #627, #629), render debt cleared end-to-end at `6.9`–`9.0` h owed (`0.41`–`0.57` h after pull request creation).
- **Negative**: ADR-0252's consequence statement ("appears 6 h after the first unrendered fragment") holds only under uninterrupted hourly delivery; across `2026-11-10`..`2026-11-16`, scheduled render pull requests opened at up to `14.0` h owed (episode `832b020a4a35`, pull request #605, `7.04` h lost to dropped windows) with a measured worst-case bound of `14.92` h (`6 h + 8.92 h` max schedule gap, plus up to `21.97` min runner wait on run `43785808315`). A schedule gap above `14` h after a fragment reaches `6` h owed lets ADR-0251 fail other pull requests at `20` h owed (and above `18` h lets ADR-0245 fail pushes at `24` h owed).
- **Neutral / follow-ups**: across the six owed episodes in `2026-11-10`..`2026-11-16` ([digest Table B and Table D](../research/0631-render-schedule-delivery.md)), breaches of `20` h and `24` h came from merge wait rather than schedule delivery:
  - Episode `38861a659e50` (PR #595): `4.91` h lost to dropped windows before run `42856098380` (`11.9` h owed, failed `--check-settings` pre-setup), `1.70` h setup wait until `workflow_dispatch` run `42873719398` opened PR #595 at `13.6` h owed (`2026-11-10T18:17:59Z`), and `0.55` h merge wait after `plurano-merge-train` disabled auto-merge (`manually_disabled`, `+11.1` min after arm at `18:29:07Z`) before `lusoris` merged at `2026-11-10T18:50:59Z` (`14.1` h owed).
  - Episode `832b020a4a35` (PR #605; `ci.yml` push run `43371294722` failed step `Changelog render owed at most 24 h` at `25.6` h owed): `7.04` h lost to dropped windows before scheduled run `43272459556` opened PR #605 at `14.0` h owed (`2026-11-12T17:43:53Z`), followed by `12.96` h merge wait until `lusoris` merged at `2026-11-13T06:41:40Z` (`27.0` h owed). `plurano-merge-train` disabled auto-merge (`manually_disabled`) after all three `vmafx-release-bot` arms (`+3.2` min at `2026-11-12T17:47:10Z`, `+43.3` min at `2026-11-13T00:34:37Z` after run `43332274952`, and `+30.9` min at `2026-11-13T06:22:32Z` after `workflow_dispatch` run `43374734216`), holding PR #605 open while commit `7b7a7955cb4b` (#603) pushed to `master` and failed the 24 h push check in `ci.yml` run `43371294722` at `25.6` h owed.
  - Episode `fbb94fc97747` (PR #613): `0.99` h lost to dropped windows before scheduled run `43454983985` opened PR #613 at `7.7` h owed (`2026-11-13T15:41:54Z`), followed by `17.32` h merge wait until `lusoris` merged at `2026-11-14T09:00:49Z` (`25.0` h owed) after `plurano-merge-train` disabled auto-merge (`manually_disabled`) on all three arms (`+4.9` min at `15:46:51Z`, `+9.0` min at `21:46:43Z` after run `43512346646`, and `+2.5` min at `2026-11-14T01:46:22Z` after run `43542298866`).
  - Episodes `362d1ef37680` (PR #625, run `43662311434`), `e202811270a4` (PR #627, run `43732159410`), and `3c7da78c7ecb` (PR #629, run `43785808315`): `1.59` h, `0.17` h, and `1.68` h lost to dropped or late windows (plus `21.97` min runner wait on `43785808315`), opening at `8.6` h, `6.4` h, and `8.4` h owed; auto-merge was never disabled on these pull requests, and `vmafx-release-bot` merged them in `0.41` h, `0.50` h, and `0.57` h (`9.0` h, `6.9` h, and `9.0` h owed).
