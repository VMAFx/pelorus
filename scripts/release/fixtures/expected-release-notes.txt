### Highlights

- Zero-free COBS side-data carrier in interop ABI 1.5 keeps flat analysis maps intact through `h264_nvenc` and `hevc_nvenc`.
- Per-cell banding, variance, and edge-density maps from `pelorus_analyze_vulkan` feed perceptual pooling directly.
- Hardware encode recipes avoid `hwdownload` across NVENC, VAAPI, QSV, and Vulkan Video encoders.
