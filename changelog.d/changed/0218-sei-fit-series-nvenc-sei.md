- The NVENC SEI fit helper (`pelorus_sei_fit.h`, patch 0022) includes `libavcodec/nvenc_sei.h` from the shared FFmpeg fix series, now pinned at `v0.3.0`, instead of keeping its own copy of the 1024-byte limit, the reserve and the emulation-prevention scan; fit decisions are unchanged. The fast suite reads the header from the fetched series through the new Meson option `ffmpeg_series_dir` (`FFMPEG_SERIES_DIR` for `make`) and reports a named skip without it; CI requires the test and a planted divergence ([ADR-0218](docs/adr/0218-sei-fit-includes-nvenc-sei.md)).
