- The Pelorus Vulkan filters now refuse input layouts they were not measured on, with `<filter>: input format <name> is not supported; supported: <list>`. Accepted: planar and semi-planar YUV at 4:2:0, 4:2:2 and 4:4:4 with 8, 10, 12 and 16 bits, plus `yuva420p`; aa, dehalo, deblock and denoise also accept `rgba`. Refused: `yuyv422` (which used to hang), `gbrp` and `gray` (which gave wrong output), alpha layouts other than `yuva420p`, and `rgba` in the other filters. Convert in the graph first, for example `scale_vulkan=format=nv12` ([#188](https://github.com/VMAFx/pelorus/issues/188), [ADR-0201](docs/adr/0201-vulkan-input-format-allow-list.md)).
- deband, denoise, mc, dehalo, aa and deblock now refuse interlaced frames (`AV_FRAME_FLAG_INTERLACED`) with `EINVAL`, because their neighbourhood reads would mix the two fields. Deinterlace first (`bwdif_vulkan`) or mark progressive frames with `setfield=prog`. analyze, grain_estimate, borderfix and scenecut keep accepting interlaced frames ([ADR-0201](docs/adr/0201-vulkan-input-format-allow-list.md)).
