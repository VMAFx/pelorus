- The NVENC SEI fit helper (`pelorus_sei_fit.h`, patches 0022 and 0023) keeps its own limit, reserve, NAL size, emulation-prevention scan and truncation rule instead of calling `nvenc_sei.h` from the shared FFmpeg fix series, which FFmpeg's merged size check replaces. On `hevc_nvenc` the budget also covers FFmpeg's 512-byte payload limit (524 bytes of NAL room on an empty picture), and patch 0022 strips the per-cell maps first and then drops a carrier that still does not fit, with a warning, instead of leaving it to FFmpeg's silent drop. The fast suite runs the SEI fit test without the series; the Meson option `ffmpeg_series_dir` is gone, and five planted variants must fail ([ADR-0236](docs/adr/0236-sei-fit-merged-form.md)).
