- Interop ABI 1.5: the zero-free carrier form of the side-data blob (UUID `3f9b37b8-fd9a-4621-920e-9b78b55cf9b5`, then the COBS-encoded blob, with no zero byte), `pel_blob_carrier_encode()` and `pel_blob_unwrap()`; no section or field changes. A reader of decoded frames calls `pel_blob_unwrap()` before `pel_blob_find_section()`; a reader built on ABI 1.4 ignores the carrier. The conformance fixture adds a known-answer vector, boundaries and malformed carriers.
