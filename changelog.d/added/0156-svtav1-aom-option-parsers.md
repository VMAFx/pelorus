- Option-string parsers for SVT-AV1 (`PEL_ENCODE_OPTIONS_SVTAV1`, pinned to `v4.2.0`, 121 entries) and libaom (`PEL_ENCODE_OPTIONS_AOM`, pinned to `v3.15.2`, 122 entries) in `pel_encode_options_parse`, following the functions FFmpeg hands `-svtav1-params` and `-aom-params` to: exact names, SVT-AV1 aliases `w`/`h`/`q`, base-0 integers for SVT-AV1 and base-10 for libaom, and negative values for unsigned options kept in `unknown_params` ([#156](https://github.com/VMAFx/pelorus/issues/156), [ADR-0198](docs/adr/0198-encoder-option-string-parsers.md)).
