- `pelorus_analyze_vulkan` writes per-cell banding risk (`uint8`), luma variance (`float`) and edge density (`uint8`) maps into the `PEL_SEC_BANDING` and `PEL_SEC_VARIANCE` offset/size fields that ABI 1.0 defines, so VMAFx's perceptual pooling reads real maps instead of falling back to frame scalars; new options `cell` (power of two 8..64, default 32) and `maps` (default 1); grids above 2^20 cells are refused at link setup; no ABI change ([ADR-0177](docs/adr/0177-analyze-per-cell-maps.md), #219).
