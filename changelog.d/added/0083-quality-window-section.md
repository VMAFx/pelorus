- Interop section `PEL_SEC_QUALITY` (bit 10, section k, 96 bytes) and `enum pel_quality_metric` in ABI 1.6: carries time-aligned quality window scores from external oracles (VMAFx); `pel_quality_align` provides exact rational PTS matching via 64-bit integer cross-multiplication with nearest-neighbour joins strictly forbidden; `pel_zones_x265_zonefile`, `pel_zones_x265_param` (with `b=`), and `pel_zones_segment_plan` format contiguous shot sequences into encoder-supported rate controls. See [docs/api/quality-window.md](docs/api/quality-window.md) and [ADR-0214](docs/adr/0214-quality-window-section.md). Part of [#83](https://github.com/VMAFx/pelorus/issues/83).
