- The AV1 film grain descriptor (`pel_grain_describe_aom`) now models the integer rounding of reference synthesis (`round2(scaling * grain, scaling_shift)`), so it no longer underpredicts low-amplitude grain (by up to 13.5% for 4:2:0 chroma on the conformance fixture). The conversions still emit bit-identical parameters; their `fidelity` output changes because it now compares the rounded AV1 descriptor with the floor H.274 descriptor ([ADR-0217](docs/adr/0217-av1-grain-round-quantization.md), [#384](https://github.com/VMAFx/pelorus/issues/384)).
