- Pelorus side data reaches the decoder intact through `h264_nvenc` and `hevc_nvenc` on flat content (#284). NVENC writes a SEI payload truncated when it needs more emulation prevention bytes than about a third of its length, which the zero maps of `pelorus_analyze_vulkan` reach, and the decoder drops it without an error. Patch 0022 now writes every Pelorus blob in a zero-free carrier form (interop ABI 1.5), and `hevc_nvenc` charges its budget for the carrier, so small flat grids keep their maps. ([ADR-0183](docs/adr/0183-sidedata-zero-free-carrier.md), [research 0183](docs/research/0183-sidedata-zero-free-carrier.md))
