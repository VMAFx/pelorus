- A new table-driven argument parser in `pelorus_tool_common` unifies command-line option parsing across `pelorus_qp_report`, `pelorus_zones`, and `pelorus_qloop`, replacing their hand-written loops with strict full-string validation, bounds checking, and duplicate/unknown flag detection. `pelorus_qp_report` now rejects trailing characters in `--requested-qp` (such as `1x`), refuses `--requested-qp` at the end of argv without a value instead of mistaking it for the positional CSV path, and refuses unexpected extra positional arguments ([#496](https://github.com/VMAFx/pelorus/issues/496)).
