- `pelorus_qp_report`, `pelorus_qloop` and `pelorus_zones` now accept only plain decimal numbers in options, history lines and `shots.csv` fields. Hex such as `0x20`, a leading `+`, a leading blank and numbers longer than 128 characters are refused instead of being read as another number; `pelorus_qp_report --requested-qp 0x20` used to report a requested QP of 32.00. A blank between a comma and the `shots.csv` CRF field is now refused too ([#583](https://github.com/VMAFx/pelorus/issues/583)).
