- `hevc_qsv` and `h264_qsv` with `-udu_sei 1` no longer damage the encoded access unit or fail the encode on large Pelorus side data (#286, patch 0023). The QSV runtime reserves a fixed SEI space per picture (4 107 bytes on `hevc_qsv`, about 42 420 on `h264_qsv`, measured on an Arc A380 with `iHD` 26.3.5); the encoders now keep 4 040 and 40 960 bytes per picture, write a Pelorus blob that does not fit without its per-cell maps, and skip any other payload that does not fit, with a warning naming the frame and sizes. At 1080p the `cell=32` analyze blob reaches an `hevc_qsv` stream as its scalar sections. Known issue, not fixed in Pelorus: `h264_qsv` with `udu_sei` (and FFmpeg's own A/53 caption path) can crash intermittently inside Intel's QSV runtime (it reproduces with stock FFmpeg, so Pelorus does not cause it; a fix is being worked on outside Pelorus), more often with larger payloads (2 KB none in 80 runs, 16 KB about 12 to 15 in 80, 30 KB 11 to 30 in 80); the budget does not prevent it ([research 0286](docs/research/0286-qsv-udu-sei-budget.md)).
