- The SEI fit helpers behind `hevc_nvenc`, `h264_qsv` and `hevc_qsv` (patches 0022 and 0023) no longer let a size sum wrap near `SIZE_MAX`: the message, NAL and carrier lengths saturate at `SIZE_MAX`, so a payload whose length is close to the top of `size_t` is reported as too large instead of as fitting the budget. A planted twin without the saturation fails the new boundary test ([#389](https://github.com/VMAFx/pelorus/issues/389)).
