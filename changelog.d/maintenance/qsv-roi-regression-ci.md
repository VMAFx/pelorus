- CI now runs the QSV ROI regression `ffmpeg-patches/test/qsv-roi-regression.sh` in the `ffmpeg-stack` job: the ASan/UBSan dense-map test and the MBQP-absent compile gate added with the `EnableMBQP` warning had never run in CI. The job installs `clang` and `libclang-rt-dev` for the sanitizer toolchain.
- The new check `scripts/check-ci-test-wiring.py` fails when a `*.sh`, `*.py` or `*.c` program under `ffmpeg-patches/test/` is named by no workflow, composite action or Meson build file, directly or through another wired test file. Its self-test plants wired, transitive, commented-out and orphaned programs plus live, expired and wrong-rule `.standards.yaml` exceptions; it runs in `make docs-check` and the CI docs job.
