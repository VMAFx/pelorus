- The interop magic, ABI major and header size are now derived from `libpelorus/include/pelorus/interop.h` instead of typed in each consumer. `tools/tester/pelorus_interop_consts.py` parses them for the tester and `ffmpeg-patches/test/analyze-maps-check.py`; the FFmpeg patch sources use named mirrors (`PEL_SEI_*` in `pelorus_sei_fit.h`, `PEL_FG_*` and `PEL_*` in the NVENC patches), and `scripts/test-nvenc-me-hints.py` builds its blob from them. The fast suite checks the mirrors with static assertions (`interop-constants`) and runs `scripts/check-interop-constants.py`, which compares every mirror with `interop.h` and refuses a hand-typed magic literal outside `interop.h`, a mirror definition or a listed allow-list; it builds its patterns from the parsed magic and reports a mirror `#define` with trailing code. The tester finds `interop.h` in a checkout or in the installed image layout (`<prefix>/include/pelorus/interop.h` next to `<prefix>/tester`), and its tool digest now covers `pelorus_interop_consts.py` and `pelorus_json_shape.py`; a tester self-test refuses a loaded sibling module the digest leaves out (#500, #515).
