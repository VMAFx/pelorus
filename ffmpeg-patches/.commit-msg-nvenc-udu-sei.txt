fix(nvenc): keep hevc_nvenc udu_sei within NVENC's per-picture header limit

hevc_nvenc with udu_sei=1 stops the encode with "Failed locking bitstream
buffer: out of memory" (AVERROR(ENOMEM)) once a picture's SEI is large.
NVENC's HEVC encoder accepts at most 1024 bytes of non-VCL NAL units per
picture: VPS, SPS, PPS, access unit delimiter and every SEI NAL unit, in
Annex B form with start codes and emulation prevention bytes. Above that,
nvEncLockBitstream() returns NV_ENC_ERR_OUT_OF_MEMORY. At the end of a
stream the same picture is lost with only an error line. h264_nvenc
carries 64 KiB payloads. Neither nvEncodeAPI.h 13.1 nor the NVENC
programming guide 13.1 states the limit.

A pelorus_analyze_vulkan blob with its per-cell maps is 12 424 bytes at
1080p with 32-pixel cells, so every analyze graph into hevc_nvenc with
udu_sei failed.

For HEVC, budget 768 bytes per picture for the SEI passed in
seiPayloadArray, less the A/53 and timecode SEI already queued, and keep
256 bytes for the NAL units NVENC writes on its own (128 bytes measured at
8K Main10 with VUI and AUD). Each SEI_UNREGISTERED entry, in frame order:

- fits: written unchanged;
- a Pelorus blob that does not fit: written without the per-cell maps
  appended after its sections. Every scalar stays, and each map offset and
  size is zero, which the Pelorus ABI defines as an absent map. The result
  is byte-identical to the analyze filter's maps=0 blob;
- anything else that does not fit: not written.

The first stripped blob and the first dropped payload are warnings, later
ones verbose, and the totals are a warning when the encoder closes. The
size arithmetic lives in libavcodec/pelorus_sei_fit.h, which the Pelorus
fast suite tests against the libpelorus packer.

Measured on an RTX 4090, driver 615.71.09: the largest payload that
encodes is 898 bytes on an IDR picture and 999 bytes on a P picture at
1080p, which is 1024 bytes of non-VCL NAL units in both cases; two
payloads fit 495 bytes each, an all-zero payload 747 bytes, and -aud 1
lowers the limit by the 7-byte delimiter. With this change, analyze maps=1
into hevc_nvenc -udu_sei 1 encodes at 1080p and at 8192x8192 with 8-pixel
cells, and the decoded stream carries the scalar sections on every picture.
