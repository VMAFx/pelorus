fix(nvenc): write Pelorus side data zero-free and within NVENC's header limit

Builds on patches 0003 and 0004 of the shared FFmpeg fix series
(VMAFx/ffmpeg-patches, applied before this stack, ADR-0185). They handle
two NVENC defects for every user data unregistered SEI with udu_sei=1:

1. 0003: hevc_nvenc fails a picture whose non-VCL NAL units (VPS, SPS,
PPS, access unit delimiter and every SEI NAL unit, in Annex B form)
exceed 1024 bytes. The SEI passed in seiPayloadArray get 768 bytes per
picture, less the A/53 and timecode SEI, and a payload that does not fit
is not written.

2. 0004: h264_nvenc and hevc_nvenc write a SEI payload truncated, without
its trailing bits, when its emulation prevention bytes exceed
ceil(P / 3) + 3 for a P-byte payload (rule pinned on an RTX 4090, driver
615.78.08), and FFmpeg's decoders then drop it. Such a payload is not
written.

Both would lose Pelorus side data. The zero runs of a
pelorus_analyze_vulkan blob on flat content reach rule 2: at 1080p with
32-pixel cells (12 424 bytes) no picture kept its side data. At real
frame sizes the analyze maps do not fit the 768 bytes of rule 1.

This patch adds one call between the series' av_memdup() of each
SEI_UNREGISTERED entry and nvenc_hevc_sei_fits():

- Every Pelorus blob (UUID e1d7c4a2-6b93-4f08-9a55-0f3c2db17e64, magic
  PELOR1, ABI major 1) is replaced by its zero-free carrier form of the
  Pelorus interop ABI 1.5: UUID 3f9b37b8-fd9a-4621-920e-9b78b55cf9b5,
  then the COBS encoding of the blob image. It holds no zero byte, so
  its NAL unit needs no emulation prevention and 0004 never drops it;
  it costs one byte plus one per 254 bytes. libpelorus's
  pel_blob_unwrap() turns it back into the blob. Any other payload is
  left as copied.
- On hevc_nvenc, a blob whose carrier does not fit the budget left in
  the picture first loses the per-cell maps appended after its sections.
  Every scalar stays, and each map offset and size is zero, which the
  Pelorus ABI defines as an absent map. The blob is byte-identical to the
  analyze filter's maps=0 blob before it becomes a carrier.
  nvenc_hevc_sei_fits() then charges the carrier or drops it.

The maps are stripped in the copy, and the carrier is written into one
new allocation that replaces it. The first stripped blob is a warning,
later ones verbose, and the total is a warning when the encoder closes;
the first carrier is noted in the verbose log. The size arithmetic, the
stripper and the carrier encoder live in libavcodec/pelorus_sei_fit.h,
which the Pelorus fast suite tests against the libpelorus packer and
carrier encoder.

Measured on an RTX 4090, driver 615.71.09 and 615.78.08: the largest
payload that encodes is 898 bytes on an IDR picture and 999 bytes on a P
picture at 1080p, which is 1024 bytes of non-VCL NAL units in both cases.
48 analyze blobs (320x180 to 1920x1080, flat, gradient and detailed
content, maps on and off): 11 of 48 lost every picture's side data on
h264_nvenc as written before, none as carriers, up to 49 145 bytes.
