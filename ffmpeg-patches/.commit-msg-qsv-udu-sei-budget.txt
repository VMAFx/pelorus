fix(qsv): budget udu_sei per picture on hevc_qsv and h264_qsv

With udu_sei=1, hevc_qsv and h264_qsv handed the runtime every
SEI_UNREGISTERED entry whole, while the runtime accepts a limited
amount of user data SEI per picture. A Pelorus analyze blob at 1080p
with 32-pixel cells (12 424 bytes) exceeded it on hevc_qsv.

The limit is per picture, not per payload: the sum of the
mfxPayload.BufSize values (SEI message type, size bytes and payload)
passed in one mfxEncodeCtrl. h264_qsv also counts the emulation
prevention bytes of the payloads. Per picture budget:

- hevc_qsv: 4 040 bytes.
- h264_qsv: 40 960 bytes.

The payloads the stock A/53 callback already queued are charged first.
Each SEI_UNREGISTERED entry, in frame order, is then:

- fits: written whole;
- a Pelorus blob that does not fit: written without the per-cell maps
  appended after its sections (every scalar stays, each map offset and
  size is zero, the ABI's absent map), as hevc_nvenc does (ADR-0181);
- anything else that does not fit: not written.

QSV writes a Pelorus blob in its plain form; the zero-free carrier is
for NVENC. The first stripped blob and the first dropped payload are
warnings, later ones verbose, and the totals are a warning at close. The
budget arithmetic is libavcodec/pelorus_sei_fit_qsv.h, built on
pelorus_sei_fit.h from patch 0022, and tested by the Pelorus fast suite.

Measured on an Arc A380 (iHD 26.3.5, vpl-gpu-rt 26.3.5, libvpl 2.17.0),
at 640x360, 1920x1080 and 3840x2160 alike. Depends on patches 0019 and
0022.
