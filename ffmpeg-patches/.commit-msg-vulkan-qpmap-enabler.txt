avutil/hwcontext_vulkan: enable VK_KHR_video_encode_quantization_map

Add the extension to the optional device extensions and chain, copy and
enable its videoEncodeQuantizationMap feature, so a device FFmpeg
creates turns both on when the driver supports them. An encode session
created with a VK_VIDEO_SESSION_CREATE_ALLOW_ENCODE_*_MAP flag needs the
feature as well as the extension
(VUID-VkVideoSessionCreateInfoKHR-flags-10264).
