vulkan: don't release frames to VK_QUEUE_FAMILY_IGNORED

On a device with a single queue family, hwcontext_vulkan creates frames
with VK_SHARING_MODE_EXCLUSIVE and tracks them as owned by that family.
ff_vk_frame_barrier() copies the tracked family into srcQueueFamilyIndex,
while every filter and codec caller passes VK_QUEUE_FAMILY_IGNORED as the
new family. The first barrier on such a frame therefore has unequal
indices, which the specification treats as a queue family ownership
transfer, and its destination is VK_QUEUE_FAMILY_IGNORED, which an
exclusive image does not allow (VUID-VkImageMemoryBarrier2-image-09118).

The Khronos validation layer accepts the barrier but records it as a
release that is never acquired. It does not apply the layout transition
and reports every later use of the image against the old layout:
VUID-vkCmdDispatch-imageLayout-00344 and VUID-vkCmdDraw-None-09600 for
shaders that access the image, and
VUID-VkCopyMemoryToImageInfo-dstImageLayout-09059 and
VUID-VkCopyImageToMemoryInfo-srcImageLayout-09064 for the host image
copies in hwupload and hwdownload.

When the caller passes VK_QUEUE_FAMILY_IGNORED and the frame is owned by
a concrete queue family, keep that family as the destination. Equal
indices perform no transfer, and the tracked owner stays the family that
hwcontext_vulkan uses for its own transitions on such devices. Frames on
devices with several queue families are tracked as
VK_QUEUE_FAMILY_IGNORED and are unaffected, as are frames owned by the
external or foreign queue family.

Tested with Mesa lavapipe, which has one queue family, and validation
layer 1.4.363: gblur_vulkan and scdet_vulkan between hwupload and
hwdownload no longer report the four layout errors. Intel ANV, RADV and
the NVIDIA proprietary driver report the same messages as before.
